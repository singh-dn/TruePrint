# India map source

`public/india-network-outline.svg` is projected from Natural Earth's public-domain 1:10m India-viewpoint country geometry, including its island polygons. It is not an official Survey of India map. The marker locations are illustrative city coordinates and do not represent company offices.

Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_admin_0_countries_ind.geojson

Source Git blob: `852f3fdf92c01811d433e258b1475179184bd9b0` (retrieved 2026-09-12).

License: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Projection (600 × 660 viewBox):

- x = 18.44134268145433 + (longitude − 68.143403) × 0.9271838545667874 × 20.78595112536624
- y = 15 + (37.054484 − latitude) × 20.78595112536624

The SVG retains all 35 source polygons. The rendered dotted treatment and connection animations are presentation layers over that geometry.
